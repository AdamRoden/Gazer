# Speech composer

Drawer **Speak** (`compose.open`) opens `compose.xml`. This is an internal phrase, not OS typing.

- Type on the composer keyboard into a buffer. Looking at a word, a run of letters and digits, waits 200 ms, then the word turns bold and grows by half over the next 600 ms. While it is bold its dwell band is three line-heights tall. Completing the look selects the word, places the caret at the end of it, and loads suggestions for it. A word already inside the selection cannot be selected. With Shift held, or while the Speak shift layer is up, looking at a word outside the selection extends the selection across both words and the text between them. A copy button sits with its bottom on the top of the phrase while text is selected. Its border is the accent color at the same thickness as the Speak buttons. Dwelling Copy copies the selection, shows a green check, and then the button disappears until the selection changes. The row under the phrase keeps undo and redo on the left and Home, a left arrow, a right arrow, and End on the right. Suggestions sit between them while word predictions are on. Those keys repeat while you look at them. Without Shift they move the caret and clear any selection. With Shift, the arrows extend or shrink the selection by one character, and Home and End extend it to that end of the phrase. Dwell a suggestion to insert it.
- Word predictions use a sentence-sized model shipped with Gazer (`resources/predict/model.bin`). A usage-ranked dictionary completes words that are not in the sample phrases. Words you accept are remembered under `%AppData%\Gazer\predict-user.bin`. Turn suggestions off under Settings → **Speak**. Undo, redo, and the caret keys stay.
- **Speak** synthesizes the buffer. Engine is **ElevenLabs v4 Turbo** when that model, a DPAPI-stored API key, and a voice id are set; otherwise **Windows SAPI**. One untagged phrase at speed 1 and pitch 1 plays as the audio arrives. A standing style or an inline `[tag]` is repeated on each breath (split at punctuation, keeping numbers, times, and web addresses whole). Speed and pitch are applied on the PC after the audio arrives, and that clip is what History replays. Three Eleven failures latch SAPI until model, voice, or key changes.
- XML `<Speak value="Hello"/>` is always Windows SAPI (a canned cell utterance). Composer **Speak** is the command `compose.speak` and uses Settings → **Speak** (SAPI or v4 Turbo).
- **Soundboard**: pin the spoken clip onto topic cells; replay without re-synthesis. Store: `%AppData%\Gazer\` (`boards.json`, `clips/`).
- **Actions** opens pin, select all, cut, copy, paste, import, and export, then **Insert tag** under Pin, undo, redo, history, voice, and style, then the keyboard, volume down, the Speak output-device volume slider, and volume up. **Import** and **Export** sit on the top row. **Insert tag** lists saved tags twelve at a time, six across. The bottom row is Prev, Next, Add, and Back. Dwell a tag to put `[tag]` at the caret. **Add** types a new tag on the keyboard. **Import** opens the file browser in import/merge mode. **Export** opens the same browser in export mode. It opens over Speak and shows the keyboard. **Cancel** closes it and restores the previous layer. Style is a standing ElevenLabs tag using the same names as [Voice](https://github.com/AdamRoden/Voice). The style layer lists favorites (or every style when none are starred). **Edit** opens the full list, six at a time in a 2×3 grid, with star, trash, and **Add**. A new name is typed on the keyboard and starts starred. Trashing the style in use clears it. The browser title is the open folder. Under that are Back, Forward, Up, the path, **List**, and **Tiles**. **Places** lists Desktop, Documents, Downloads, Pictures, Music, This PC, and disks. **List** is one row per folder or JSON file, with the name, date, type, and size. **Tiles** shows thirty-two icons at a time, eight across. A folder opens. A JSON file selects and fills the name box. Up and down arrows and the track scroll the files. Import/merge puts **Import**, **Merge**, and **Cancel** beside the file name. Export puts the file name, then Home, Left, Right, and End, then **Save** and **Cancel**. Those keys move the caret the same way as on the phrase, including Shift. Looking at a word in the name selects it. A word past the right edge of the name is not selectable. Copy sits on the top of the name while text is selected, shows a green check, then disappears until the selection changes. Yes or No covers the name and leaves the selection and a copy check in place. Import, Merge, and Save ask **Yes** or **No** before reading or writing. Import replaces the board and applies model, speed, pitch, and volume. Merge adds to the board. Save writes an `aac-workspace` v1 file under that name. The volume slider has an arrow at each end. Look at the track until its ring fills. Looking at either end moves the thumb there at full speed, and a look partway moves it more slowly. List scrollbars do the same. The keyboard **Edit** key renames pins and topics. Speech boost stays on the voice list and under Settings → Speak.
- **History**: last 50 composed utterances; replay, restore, or delete.
- Settings → **Speak**: **Edit** opens the key board. **Paste** fills it from the clipboard. **Create key** clears the clipboard, closes Settings, opens ElevenLabs, and shows **Click** and **Cancel** above the taskbar at the menu’s width, with **Click** on the left. **Click** is a left click at gaze, so you can press Authorize in the browser. When that click leaves an `sk_` key on the clipboard, Gazer checks it and, if the key is valid, returns to Speak settings. **Cancel** returns to Speak settings. On the key board, **Clear** deletes the stored key. **Save** stores a valid key, or an empty key, and closes the board. The key is never stored in `settings.json`. The page also has model, speed, pitch, boost, and word predictions. Playback devices are full-height rows; scroll the page to reach them. The voice list can search by name and filter by any language. Search covers the phrase and shows six voice rows at the full row height so the keyboard can type the name. Pitch is ElevenLabs only. **System default** follows Windows. A named device is used for SAPI, v4 Turbo, saved clips, and the volume controls. If that device is unplugged, speech and volume use the Windows default until it returns.

While the action’s **source page** is `compose` (or a compose live board), `Send` and mapping/modifier commands stay in Gazer. Letters go into the phrase, into the file name when the file browser is on top, or into icon search. `backspace` deletes and `space` inserts a space. `enter` speaks, saves a name, or closes the icon search keyboard. `escape` stops speech, dismisses a file-browser Yes/No, or closes. `leftShift` / `tab` are consumed with no OS inject. `arrowLeft` / `arrowRight` / `home` / `end` move the phrase caret and the export file-name caret, and repeat like keyboard keys. Without Shift, arrows move one character and clear any selection. Shift, including the Speak shift layer, extends the selection. Caps uses XML `ShowLayers`. The full QWERTY board (`qwerty_main`) still types into Windows. Capture table: [Actions — Composer capture](reference/actions.md#composer-capture).

Composer commands (`compose.*`, `speech.*`, `soundboard.*`, `history.*`) are in the [command catalog](reference/commands.md). `compose.moveStartOfWord`, `compose.selectWord`, `compose.moveEndOfWord`, and `compose.pageChips` stay registered and are not on the Speak board.
